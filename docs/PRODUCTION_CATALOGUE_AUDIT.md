## Scheduling and delivery findings

- The four recurring hourly cron entries and three supplementary expressions in `.github/workflows/telegram-daily.yml` feed a serialized publisher with a 155-minute job limit and an in-job sleep. Scheduled capacity is shared. The status journal cannot measure the delays caused specifically by Actions.
- Telegram spacing is 45 minutes. In `scheduled_dispatch.main`, the official snapshot and research hooks for editions 5, 10 and 14 run before the dispatcher's spacing check. In `run`, a cooldown returns before ordinary work is enumerated and resets the due/generated counters to zero. A zero on that path cannot establish that the queue was empty.
- The last dispatch record shows the price prediction targeting 18:00 Eastern on 27 November for the 00:00 UTC update on 28 November. MONITOR at 17:30 and SELL at 18:30 triggered the collision rule. No publication ID was recorded for this opportunity. Historical failed confirmed-price-change posts belong to the retired edition.
- The autonomous schedule caps retries at three. Regular catch-up uses today's Eastern date. Weekly analysis pins a completed GW, then selects the series day using elapsed dates. Either scheme can abandon an overdue opportunity. Some Saturday work is scheduled at 22:30, outside the proposed 21:00 close.
- Seven series identities are currently tied to a completed GW. The proposed 43 weekly / eight dynamic split requires a deliberate change to these seven triggers or a different split. Four pre-match specials are timed from opening kickoff and also gated by the official deadline. Preserve both semantics when moving a fixture.
- The PR workflow runs pytest but tolerates BUY/SELL preview errors and missing uploaded files. It does not prove all 51 render. This audit changes documentation only.
- By audit timestamp in Eastern time, excluding RUN summaries, the saved audit contains 146 POSTED, eight FAILED, 13 CHECKED and one RETRY. By completion timestamp, only 125 POSTED rows fall inside this interval. Neither figure is unique edition coverage.
- Recorded blockers include Understat structured data for Chance Creators, an unavailable Top 100k sample, absent bookmaker win probabilities for Wednesday Market Outlook and uncertain Telegram acceptance for Saturday Afternoon Rotation. Preserve uncertain sends for reconciliation. The outbox journal also records stale editions held to prevent replay.
- No receipt pair was attributable for GW12 Press Round-Up at the snapshot. Its journal tracks timetable and facts separately from platform message IDs. Final gameweek news was still listed as a future trigger. Missing evidence for either edition is insufficient to declare missed delivery.

## Data and rendering paths

Legacy editions use `vortex.bot`, `vortex.content` and `vortex.render`. The official FPL snapshot feeds the fixture guide, statistical discoveries and transfer candidates. MONITOR and SELL share recommendation code. Editorial editions use `autonomous_content.build` and `autonomous_render.render` via `autonomous_runner`, then send through the outbox. The source-to-plan dispatch is keyed by the permanent edition or its rotation parent. Player Radar uses the comparison builder. The ownership board needs a cohort sample, the Wednesday afternoon odds board needs bookmaker probabilities, and the value board checks fixture-level model coverage. Scouting topics can switch to labelled fallback metrics depending on archive coverage. The six pre/post-match specials depend on official fixture and event state. Price analysis additionally depends on the official update clock and progress observations.

The rotation news route selects official fixture data in the unified runner, renders with the rotation planner and records shared platform receipts. The press route uses conference discovery, verified source reports, the press renderer and per-GW message IDs for edits. Per-platform state lives in the legacy, ChatGPT and news delivery journals and the press state. Outbox status, an audit status and a process exit code do not independently verify platform acceptance. The repository snapshot cannot establish current platform visibility, follower conversion or predictive accuracy.
